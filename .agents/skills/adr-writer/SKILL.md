# ADR Writer

Criteria, naming, and numbering rules: [`docs/adr/README.md`](../../../docs/adr/README.md). Skip ADRs
for routine, easily reversed choices.

Use [`docs/templates/ADR-template.md`](../../../docs/templates/ADR-template.md). Include context, the
decision, why now, alternatives considered, consequences (positive and tradeoffs), operational impact
(migration, rollback, monitoring), follow-ups, and references to the brief, plan, or issue.
